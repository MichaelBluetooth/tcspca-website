Board of Directors

Meet our Board of Directors!

Our volunteer Board of Directors guides the shelter’s mission, finances, and long-term planning so we can keep caring for animals and finding them loving homes. Board members bring a wide range of experience and a shared commitment to animal welfare in our community. We welcome questions, ideas, and feedback. You can reach the board directly at tcspcaboard@gmail.com
