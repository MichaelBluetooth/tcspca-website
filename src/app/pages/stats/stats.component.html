Every Nose Counts

Statistics

Since 1999, the SPCA of Tompkins County has been collecting and sharing data on the animals in our care. Every animal who comes through our doors is counted, and that information helps us make their stay more comfortable, reduce the spread of disease, track length of stay, and understand how the shelter population has changed over time.

Our data does a lot of work behind the scenes. It supports research, strengthens our grant applications, keeps Tompkins County and our Board of Directors informed, and guides us as we review and improve how we care for animals.

Shelter Animals Count

We also submit our data to Shelter Animals Count, a national database that tracks shelter and rescue statistics across the country. We encourage you to visit their site to see how our numbers fit into the bigger picture.
