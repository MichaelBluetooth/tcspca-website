Free rabies vaccinations for dogs, cats, and ferrets.

The SPCA, in conjunction with the Tompkins County Health Department, helps facilitate free rabies clinics to members of our community. The clinics are held in the Winter, Spring and Fall and are held at least once in each individual municipality in Tompkins County. Members of the public can bring their Dog, Cat, or Ferret for vaccination with no appointment, however wait times at individual clinics may vary. For more information and exact locations of free rabies clinics in Tompkins County visit the Health Department's website.
