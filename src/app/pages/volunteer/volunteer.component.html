Interested in becoming a volunteer? Awesome!

Volunteers are the heart of the SPCA of Tompkins County. We ask all volunteers to support and promote our mission and to follow the shelter's written volunteer policies, which you'll receive at orientation. Every assignment is arranged between you and our Volunteer Coordinator to make sure it's the best fit for you and for the animals.

Age and training requirements vary by role, so please review the opportunities below before you apply.

Steps to becoming a volunteer

  1. Apply. Complete and submit a volunteer application (Adult Application / Youth Application). Within two weeks, our Volunteer Coordinator will email you with the status of your application.
  2. Attend a Zoom orientation. Once we've identified a volunteer need that matches your interests, we'll contact you to schedule your orientation.
  3. Complete online training. After orientation, you'll complete our online training, which covers the basics every volunteer needs before working at the shelter.
  4. Attend in-person training. Finally, you'll be scheduled for a one-on-one training session with an experienced volunteer. Sessions take 1–2 hours, depending on your position.

What we ask of our volunteers

  • Time commitment: Most assignments require a minimum of 2–3 hours, or one shift, per week. Scheduling is flexible, but we appreciate a commitment of 8–12 hours per month for at least 6 months (one semester for college students).
  • Required training: New York State law requires animal shelters to train all volunteers who have direct animal care responsibilities within their first 60 days and at least annually after that (NYS Agriculture & Markets Law §422). Volunteers in these roles must complete their initial training on time and complete refresher training each year to keep volunteering.
  • Transportation: A car or other reliable transportation is highly recommended.
  • Policies: All volunteers must follow the shelter's written volunteer policies, provided at orientation.

Dog volunteer opportunities

Canine Companion (minimum age 18)

Canine Companions spend their time indoors with our dogs. You'll help visitors meet dogs and find their new friend, play with dogs in the play yard, give plenty of one-on-one attention in their rooms, and do some light cleaning if a dog has an accident during the day.

Dog Walker (minimum age 18)

Dog Walkers do just that: walk our dogs for exercise. You'll also provide enrichment using appropriate equipment, help teach basic obedience, and clean up after the dogs on walks, in the play yard, and in their rooms. At times you'll talk with visitors and help them find their new best friend.

Purple Dog Walker (minimum age 18, plus additional training)

Purple Dog Walkers do the same work as Dog Walkers, with additional training to handle dogs who are exceptionally large or strong, who need extra training, or who are on a behavior modification plan.

Cat volunteer opportunities

Feline Friend (minimum age 12)

Feline Friends spend quality time with our cats, socializing them and helping keep their apartments clean. You'll also help visitors find a cat that's purr-fect for their home.

Animal care opportunities (all animals)

Animal Care Attendant (minimum age 18)

This role is for dedicated volunteers who understand how important a clean environment is to the health of our animals. Animal Care Attendants help clean and care for dogs, cats, and other animals throughout the shelter, including in our rescue center, which houses newly arrived animals, animals under observation, and animals receiving medical care. Daily cleaning prevents the spread of disease and keeps every animal healthy and comfortable. If you're up for it, sign up today!

Other volunteer opportunities

Interested in something else? Download a volunteer application (Adult Application / Youth Application) and note your areas of interest. Here are some of our most common opportunities.

Facility Assistant (minimum age 16)

Love fixing things around the house? Put your skills to work on projects throughout our buildings and grounds. Our shelter needs constant maintenance, and your help creates a better environment for animals, visitors, volunteers, and staff.

Medical Assistant / Surgery Recovery (minimum age [TBD])

Help our medical team care for animals before and after surgery. Medical Assistants support staff during medical care and help monitor and comfort animals as they recover from spay/neuter and other procedures. Experience with animal medical care is helpful. Tell our Volunteer Coordinator about your background and we'll get you started.

Other special skills?

Have a special talent you'd like to share? Every department can use help with special projects, events, and programs. Tell our Volunteer Coordinator what you'd like to offer, and we'll find the right way to put your skills to work for the animals.
