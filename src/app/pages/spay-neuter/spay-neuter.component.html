Low-Cost Spay/Neuter Clinic

The SPCA of Tompkins County offers a low-cost spay/neuter clinic to qualified residents of New York State, as well as residents of Tompkins and surrounding counties.

Clinics are typically held every Tuesday, as well as some Fridays. In partnership with Cornell's Maddie's® Shelter & Community Medicine, we offer several programs for spaying and neutering cats and dogs. You may qualify for one or more of our low-cost programs, including those for people on a form of public assistance. Appointments are required.

Services Offered

  • Spay/neuter surgery

With a spay/neuter surgery, your pet can also receive:

  • FVRCP (cats) or DHPP (dogs) vaccine
  • Rabies vaccine
  • Microchip
  • Flea/tick treatment

These add-on services are only available with a spay/neuter surgery and can't be booked on their own.

How to Apply

Print a spay/neuter application and mail it in, drop it off, or email it to us.

To learn more about our program, or to have an application sent to you, call (607) 257-1822 ext. 244 or email clinic@spcaonline.com.
